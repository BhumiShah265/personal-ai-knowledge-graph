# Personal Study Notes: Machine Learning & Knowledge Graphs

## Topic 1: Deep Learning Fundamentals
Neural Networks are computational systems inspired by the biological neural networks in human brains. 
They consist of layers of interconnected artificial neurons. 
Gradient Descent is an optimization algorithm used to minimize the loss function of a neural network by iteratively moving in the direction of steepest descent.
Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) and Adam are popular variants of optimization methods.
Gradient Descent relies on Backpropagation to compute the gradients of the loss function with respect to model weights.

## Topic 2: Modern Transformer Architectures
The Transformer model was introduced by Vaswani et al. in the paper "Attention Is All You Need".
Transformers rely on the Self-Attention Mechanism to weigh the importance of different words in a sequence regardless of distance.
Large Language Models (LLMs) such as GPT-4 and Llama 3 are built upon the Transformer decoder architecture.
LLMs utilize Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to ground their answers in external domain knowledge databases, preventing hallucinations.

## Topic 3: Graph Databases & Knowledge Graphs
Neo4j is an enterprise-grade native graph database management system. 
It uses the Cypher Query Language to declare complex graph patterns, nodes, and relationships.
A Knowledge Graph represents real-world entities (nodes) and their semantic connections (edges).
GraphRAG combines Knowledge Graphs with Vector Indexing to perform structured context retrieval for Large Language Models.
LlamaIndex provides robust data framework tools for connecting custom data sources to LLM applications and constructing knowledge graphs.
